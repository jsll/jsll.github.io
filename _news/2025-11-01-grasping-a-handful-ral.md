Our paper [Grasping a Handful: Sequential Multi-Object Dexterous Grasp Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.22370) was published in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L). It generates grasps that let one multi-fingered hand pick up several objects, one after another.
