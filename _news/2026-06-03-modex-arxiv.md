Our preprint [MoDex: A Diffusion Policy for Sequential Multi-Object Dexterous Grasping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05407) is now on arXiv. The policy keeps adjusting the grasp to what the robot sees, so that a multi-fingered robot hand can pick up several objects in a row.
