## 🧑‍🎓 Student supervision

I have co-supervised **3 PhD students** at KTH, two of whom graduated in 2025, and was principal supervisor of **11 MSc theses** at Aalto University, KTH and the Czech Technical University. Their projects covered how robots choose where to grasp an object (grasp synthesis), how they handle cloth and other soft objects, and generative models for robotics.

## 🎯 Prospective students

I am currently **not looking for PhD students**. I do supervise **Master's thesis projects** in five areas. The example papers show what such a project can look like.

- **Grasp generation for robot hands**, from two-finger grippers to multi-fingered (dexterous) hands, with the grasp's full 3D position and orientation (6-DoF). Examples: [generating multi-finger grasps with diffusion models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02989) and [grasping several objects in one hand](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.22370).
- **Manipulating cluttered scenes and deformable objects** such as cloth. Examples: [multi-finger grasping in clutter from a single depth image](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.04783), [tracking the 3D shape of cloth from camera images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01715) and [identifying a garment and its full shape when folds hide parts of it](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16293).
- **Generative models and planning under uncertainty**, for when the robot cannot see everything it needs to know. Example: [planning manipulation when the robot cannot tell where one object ends and the next begins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.13565).
- **Perception that combines vision and touch** (visuo-haptic perception). Example: [choosing where to touch an object to complete its 3D shape](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.09149).
- **Vision-language-action (VLA) models**, which turn camera images and written instructions into robot actions, for robots with different grippers and hands.

If one of these fits you, email me at [jens.lundell@utu.fi](mailto:jens.lundell@utu.fi) with:

1. a short paragraph on your background, and which of my [papers](/publications/) or [projects](/repositories/) interests you and why,
2. your CV and transcript,
3. links to any code or papers you have worked on.
